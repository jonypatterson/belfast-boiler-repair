Oil Boilers in Northern Ireland

Unlike much of mainland UK where gas central heating dominates, a large proportion of homes in Northern Ireland rely on oil-fired boilers for their heating and hot water.

This means it's important to use an engineer who is experienced with oil systems, not just gas. Our team works on both types regularly across Belfast and surrounding areas.

Common oil boiler brands in Belfast homes include Firebird, Grant, Worcester Greenstar Oil, and Warmflow. We repair and service all of these.

If you're not sure what type of boiler you have, just give us a call and we'll help you figure it out before booking anything in.
